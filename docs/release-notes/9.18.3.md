### Improved
- System Cleaner now adapts to the window size and resolution: text, spacing and icons scale smoothly from 1080p to 1440p and 4K (including Windows display scaling).
- Category cards are larger and show their full description; the grid uses about 3 columns at 1080p, 4 at 1440p and 5 at 4K.
- Software Health rows and section hints are easier to read and its lists use more of the window height.
- Added shared fluid size tokens to the design system for other pages to adopt.
