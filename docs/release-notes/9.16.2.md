# MYLE 9.16.2

Spotify opens automatically after successful Spicetify setup, with a simpler installation card.

## Spotify & Spicetify

- After installation, update, or repair succeeds, MYLE explicitly opens the configured Spotify Desktop installation with a visible window. A launch error preserves the completed installation and reports how to open Spotify manually.
- An up-to-date installation shows only the **Up to date** status. Extra **Check again** and **Repair / reinstall** buttons are removed; other states offer one relevant action.
- If a Spotify update removes Spicetify or Marketplace modifications, the card offers **Repair** even when both installed versions are current.
- The setup console collapses after success, stays open after a failure, and logs the actual requested action.
- Cancellation after installation has committed skips the automatic launch without undoing the completed installation.

## Downloads

- **Windows:** download **MYLE.exe** or update from MYLE.
- Browser extension 1.5.1 and MYLE Passwords 9.3.0 are unchanged.
