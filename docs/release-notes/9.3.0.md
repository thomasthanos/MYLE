## 🪟 Starting and closing MYLE

- **Keep running in the tray** is now off unless you switch it on (Settings). Off, closing the window quits MYLE; on, it stays next to the clock so Ctrl+Shift+L and browser filling keep working.
- **Start with Windows** is respected: switched off, MYLE no longer starts at sign-in, even if an older setup put its Startup shortcut back.
- Updated from an earlier version and want MYLE in the tray again? Switch **Keep running in the tray** on in Settings.

## 📱 MYLE Passwords for Android and iPhone

- See, copy and edit your logins and their 2FA codes; they sync with MYLE on your PC through your account, encrypted with your master password before they leave your devices.
- Open the vault with **Face ID, Touch ID or your fingerprint**, or the master password. It locks when you have been away, and copied passwords leave the clipboard after 30 seconds.
- **Sign in inside the app:** iPhone uses iOS's sign-in sheet (one tap if you are signed in to Discord or Google in Safari); Android shows the page over the app, and closing it cancels.
- **Scan a site's 2FA QR code** with the camera.
- **Android updates:** **Update** downloads the new version in the app and opens Android's installer: no browser, nothing left in Downloads. The first time, allow MYLE Passwords to install updates and come back.
- **iPhone:** the icon follows your Home Screen's look (light, dark or tinted).
- **Android:** the app stays clear of the status bar, the navigation bar and the keyboard.

## 🔑 Password Manager on Windows

- **Passkeys:** when a site offers one, MYLE asks **Save in MYLE?**; when it asks for one, MYLE lists yours. They sync with your vault.
- **2FA codes:** keep a site's authenticator key with its login (paste it, snip its QR code, or open a picture of it); MYLE shows the code and fills it into the site.
- **Asks to save** new logins, 2FA keys and passkeys as you enter them, even with the vault locked, and not twice for the same login.
- **Browser filling** is on by default and repairs its connection to Chrome, Edge, Brave and Firefox by itself; the extension comes from the Chrome Web Store.
- **Windows programs:** user name and password in one step, programs that update and Store apps stay linked, Steam-like sign-ins are filled right.
- The vault and the login editor fit a 1080p screen without scrolling.
