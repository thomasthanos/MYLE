# MYLE 11.0.1

### Improved
- **Steam achievements:** open a game's achievements in one window, see their descriptions and rarity, and unlock or lock one, selected achievements or all of them. Locking asks for confirmation first.
- **System Cleaner:** scan before selecting what to clean. Category headings toggle their checkbox, administrator-only folders are clearly marked, and disabled controls show when they cannot be used.
- **Windows Hello:** the vault's sign-in prompt comes to the front when opening it from the browser, with a short wait for MYLE to gain focus first.
- **Clearer controls:** shared checkboxes and tooltips behave consistently across the app, splash screen and installer.

### Fixed
- A page error stays within that page, and asynchronous failures are handled more reliably.
- Old web view caches are cleared after updates and periodically, while cookies, settings and local storage are kept.
- Steam Automation pauses status polling while its window is hidden and restricts access to its owner.
