## 🛠️ Fixes

| | What's fixed |
|---|---|
| ⬇️ **Update progress** | The ring and percentage follow the download every 100 ms, instead of sitting at 0% and jumping to 100%. |
| 📥 **Downloads cleaner** | Old zips, installers and folders are found again; desktop.ini and hidden files are no longer listed. |

## ⬇️ Update progress

- Progress is sent on a clock, not only when data arrives, so a download that comes in bursts still moves the ring.
- The percentage updates with the ring. When the server does not give a size, the size from the update manifest is used; without either, the downloaded MB show with a spinning ring.

## 📥 Downloads folder

- **Date modified** decides, as in Explorer's date groups: copying or unpacking (date created) and opening (date accessed) no longer make old files look new.
- A folder is judged by its own date modified, whatever is inside it.
- desktop.ini, Thumbs.db and hidden or system files are never listed or touched.
- **Kept, and why:** the preview has a list of what stays, with the reason (document, or modified in the last 7 days with the date).
- The card is **on by default** now. Deleting by hand still asks first and is permanent.
- **Clean Downloads automatically** (off by default): when MYLE starts, at most once a day, it deletes with the same rules without asking.
