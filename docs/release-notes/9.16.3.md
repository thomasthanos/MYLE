# MYLE 9.16.3

A small visual refresh: dark navy surfaces and lightly raised glass controls.

## Appearance

- The Dark theme uses blue-tinted navy layers, with clearer text and muted indigo accents.
- Shared buttons use translucent fills, a fine upper highlight, and soft shadows for a little depth. Button fills no longer use gradients.
- Hover, pressed, disabled, and keyboard focus states remain distinct. Ghost actions keep their quiet transparent appearance.
- Window controls, account sign-in buttons, selected tabs, and app selections follow the cleaner treatment.
- The Install Apps selection bar stays opaque, with a simple border and a restrained shadow.

## Downloads

- **Windows:** download **MYLE.exe** or update from MYLE. Includes the Spotify launch and simplified Spicetify card fixes from 9.16.2.
- Browser extension 1.5.1 and MYLE Passwords 9.3.0 are unchanged.
