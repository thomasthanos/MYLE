## 📥 Downloads folder in System Cleaner

| | What's new |
|---|---|
| 🔍 **In the scan** | **Downloads folder** is a category card like Temp files or the Recycle Bin, measured by **Scan** and ticked with the rest. |
| 🧹 **One Clean** | **Clean selected** empties it with the other categories. The confirmation says plainly that these files are deleted permanently, not to the Recycle Bin. |
| 📋 **In detail** | Below the cards: what will be deleted, and what is kept and why (document, or modified in the last 7 days). |

- The rules are the same: documents and anything modified in the last 7 days stay; hidden and system files (desktop.ini) are never touched; files in use are skipped.
- Untick the card to leave Downloads alone.
- The separate Downloads buttons and the "Clean Downloads automatically" option from 9.14.1 are gone: the normal Scan and Clean do it.
