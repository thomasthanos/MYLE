# MYLE 10.1.6

- **More reliable Disk Analyzer.** Rapid navigation, new scans and size-mode changes keep the newest results. Revealing a file waits for pending folder pages. Drive refreshes share unfinished probes, and bounded scanner queues reduce accumulated work.
- **Safer vault and account state.** Locking the vault discards obsolete decrypted replies. Overlapping loads share subscriptions, stale account responses cannot restore old access, and large-vault merging uses indexed lookups.
- **Improved backup reliability.** Restore and completeness checks respect cancellation. Cleanup preserves unrelated files, large comparison previews honor their hash limit, and incremental metadata uses a bounded cache. History and Game Saves results follow the latest settings.
- **Better Git and release operations.** Process and pipe ownership improve cancellation and cleanup. Streamed diagnostics and live problem lists have retention limits, uploads keep their final progress update, and stalled uploads remain cancellable.
- **Clearer tool output and recovery.** Short multiline and UTF-16 logs keep their content and line boundaries. Old stop confirmations cannot cancel a newer maintenance run, and Spotify releases completed jobs even when its final refresh fails.
- **Cleaner workflow improvements.** Leftover scans are faster, findings can be revealed in Explorer, and failed category scans wait for their Downloads preview before another scan starts.

The release includes the Windows installer and Chromium/Firefox extension packages. MYLE Passwords for Android and iOS remains on its existing release.
