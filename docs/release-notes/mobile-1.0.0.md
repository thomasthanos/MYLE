## MYLE Passwords 1.0.0

The Password Manager of <a href="https://github.com/thomasthanos/MYLE"><kbd>MYLE</kbd></a> on your phone: your vault from the PC, in sync both ways.

| | |
|---|---|
| 🔄 **In sync with your PC** | Sign in with the Discord or Google account you use in MYLE: your vault comes to the phone, and every change goes both ways. |
| 🔐 **Encrypted on your devices** | The vault opens only with your master password, on the phone as on the PC. Nobody else can read it, not even the account. |
| 🙂 **Face ID, Touch ID, fingerprint** | Open the vault with a glance or a touch; the master password always works too. |
| 🛡️ **2FA codes** | Each login shows its current code. Add a site's key by scanning its QR code with the camera. |
| ✏️ **Edit anywhere** | Add, edit and delete logins, generate passwords, see a login's earlier passwords and its passkeys. |
| 📋 **Copy safely** | Copied passwords and codes leave the clipboard after 30 seconds; the vault locks when you have been away. |

### Install

- **Android:** download <a href="https://downloads.thomast.uk/MYLE-Passwords.apk"><kbd>MYLE-Passwords.apk</kbd></a> on the phone and open it (allow installing from your browser when Android asks). The app tells you when a new version is out.
- **iPhone:** install <a href="https://sidestore.io"><kbd>SideStore</kbd></a>, then in SideStore add the source `https://downloads.thomast.uk/sidestore.json` and install **MYLE Passwords**. With a free Apple ID, SideStore re-signs it every 7 days.

The app shows and edits your logins; it does not fill them into other apps.
