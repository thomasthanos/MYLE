## 🔐 Passkeys

- **Unlock and carry on:** if MYLE is locked when a site asks for a passkey, MYLE's prompt says so, with **Unlock in MYLE**. Unlock MYLE (from the prompt or by yourself) and the sign-in goes on by itself. No more pressing Try again.
- **MYLE closed:** on a site where you have a passkey in MYLE, the prompt offers **Open MYLE**. It starts MYLE, and once the vault is unlocked, it signs you in.
- **Sign-in forms that offer passkeys as you type:** the menu on the user name field now opens on fields marked for passkeys too. While MYLE is locked, it offers **Unlock MYLE to use your passkey** and shows your passkeys as soon as the vault opens.
- **Found more reliably:** a site can name its passkeys in several ways (with or without padding, or in plain base64). MYLE now compares the passkeys themselves, so a site that wrote the name differently still finds yours. Site names are matched without regard to case.
- **Google sign-in:** "Use your passkey to verify it's you" now finds the passkey saved in MYLE. Google asks from a hidden helper frame of the same page, which MYLE now answers as well. MYLE also gives Google the answer browsers give about older security keys.
- To offer itself while it is locked or closed, the extension remembers which sites have a passkey in MYLE. It keeps them as salted fingerprints, never the site names or the passkeys. It learns them whenever MYLE is unlocked.

## ⚙️ Settings

- A **side menu** with sections: **Account & sync**, **Appearance**, **Startup & tray**, **Updates** and **About**. It follows as you scroll and marks where you are.
- **Search settings** (or press **Ctrl+F** or **/**): type "tray", "theme" or "update" and only what matches stays.
- **Appearance:** pick **Default** or **Dark** from two cards with a preview of each.
- Each setting says what it does in plain words, and what changes when you turn it on or off.

## 🚀 Windows Optimization

- **Quick setup** shows its three steps (**Pick**, **Review**, **Apply**) and which one you are on.
- Each profile says how safe it is: **Light** and **Recommended** are marked safe, **Maximum** is marked advanced because some Windows features turn off.
- At the bottom, MYLE shows this PC's Windows, how many settings are already optimized (with a bar), and undo for everything MYLE changed.
- In **Settings**, every line shows what it does under its name, a legend explains the marks, and settings to read first say **Read first**.
- Each tab has a line under the title that says what it is for.

## 🌑 Appearance

- **Dark theme:** buttons are solid charcoal, the main buttons use a deeper accent, and the Google sign-in button turns dark. Switches and the bar under Windows Optimization follow the theme too.
- **Scrollbars:** every scrolling list and panel uses MYLE's slim scrollbar, in both themes. Some still showed Windows' own. The browser extension's popup and menus have them too.
