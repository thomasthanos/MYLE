## 🌑 Appearance

- **Glass effects is gone.** Panels are always solid now, with no see-through or blurred surfaces.
- **Dark theme** takes its place in **Settings → Appearance**. Panels, menus, tooltips, notifications and the window turn charcoal grey-black, and the text turns neutral white. Buttons, highlights and status colours keep their colours.
- The update screen at start-up follows the dark theme too.
- With an account, the dark theme syncs to your other PCs like your other preferences.

## 🔑 Password Manager

- **Passphrases:** in the generator, choose **Passphrase**. Pick 3 to 12 words, what goes between them (`-`, `.`, `_`, `+` or nothing), and whether words get capitals and a number. The words come from the EFF's list of 7,775 easy-to-type words.
- The generator shows about how strong the result is in bits. It keeps at least one kind of character switched on, and stays smooth while you drag the slider.
- **Quick copy:** point at a login in the list to copy its user name or password without opening it. Copy buttons show a tick when they worked.
- **Search** matches every word you type, in any field: name, user name, websites, folder, notes, programs and passkeys. A clear button empties it.
- **Sort** the list by name (favorites first) or by the most recently changed. MYLE remembers your choice.
- A **2FA** filter shows the logins with 2FA codes.
- **Keyboard:** **Ctrl+F** or **/** searches, **Ctrl+N** adds a login, and the arrow keys, **Home** and **End** move through the list. In the editor, **Ctrl+S** saves and **Esc** cancels.
- **Editor:** if you cancel with unsaved changes, MYLE asks first. A new login is named after its website if you didn't name it. A note warns you when another login already has the same user name for the same website.
- An entry now says when it was changed, for example "Changed 3 days ago".
- **Fixes:** a password you show, hide and show again now stays visible for the full 30 seconds. A password you type while the editor is still loading is no longer replaced by the saved one.

## 🧩 Browser extension (1.3.0)

- **Copy from the popup:** each login has buttons for its user name, password and 2FA code. MYLE puts the text on the clipboard itself, marked as secret, and clears it after 30 seconds. The password never passes through the browser. It only works for logins saved for that website.
- The popup shows how many logins match the page, has a search icon, **/** jumps to search, and **Esc** clears it.
- While it loads, it shows placeholder rows instead of "Checking…".
- With nothing saved for a site, a button takes you to MYLE to add it.
- Copying from the popup needs MYLE 9.11.0. With an older MYLE, the popup asks you to update. Filling works as before.
