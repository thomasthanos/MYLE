## 📱 MYLE Passwords (Android & iOS)

- Available directly as **MYLE-Passwords.apk** (Android) and **MYLE-Passwords.ipa** (SideStore for iPhone).
- Unlock with biometric authentication (Face ID, Touch ID, fingerprint) or your master password.
- Read 2FA setup QR codes directly with your phone's camera.
- Clipboard auto-clearing after 30 seconds and automatic locking when navigating away.
