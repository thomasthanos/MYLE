## 🔐 Passkeys

- **MYLE closed:** when a site asks for a passkey MYLE has, MYLE starts by itself. It is started on its own, outside the browser, and if that does not bring it up, through Windows with MYLE's own `myle://` link, as a click on its shortcut would.
- **MYLE locked:** its window comes forward on the Password Manager and asks for **Windows Hello** at once (when it is on), or puts the cursor in the master password field. A line says that a sign-in in your browser is waiting.
- **Then it finishes:** with one passkey for the site, MYLE signs you in as soon as the vault opens. With several, it shows them to pick from.
- **The request stays open:** the prompt on the page waits up to three minutes and says what is happening ("Starting MYLE…", "Unlock your vault in MYLE…"), instead of giving up after a few tries.
- A sign-in field's menu never brings MYLE up by itself: only the site's own passkey request, or your click, does.

## ⚙️ Settings

- **Tabs** for Account & sync, Appearance, Startup & tray, Updates and About, with the search next to them. The tab you used last opens next time.
- Smaller rows and headings. "What is saved in your account" folds away until you open it.

## 🚀 Start screen and updates

- MYLE's icon draws in and gently breathes, inside a ring that fills as an update downloads and spins while MYLE checks.
- **Check**, **Update** and **Open** show which step the start is on. Errors turn the ring red while it counts down to opening the app.
- With **reduce motion** on in Windows, nothing moves.

## 🧩 Browser extension (1.5.0)

- Waits for MYLE to start and unlock, with the time left in its prompt, and signs in by itself after.
- The debug log also shows what MYLE is waiting for.
