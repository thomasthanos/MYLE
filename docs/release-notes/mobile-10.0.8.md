# MYLE Passwords 10.0.8

### 🔒 Face ID and Touch ID (iPhone)
- Face ID or Touch ID opens your vault only with your face or finger, never the iPhone passcode, and only as they were set up when you turned it on: a face or finger added later doesn't open it.
- If you used it before, unlock once with your master password and turn it on again in **Settings › Security & vault**. The app tells you when this is needed.

### 🛡️ Your vault stays on this phone
- **iPhone:** the app blurs itself in the app switcher, and its data stays out of iCloud and computer backups.
- **Android:** the app switcher no longer shows a picture of the vault, and the vault and your sign-in are not copied into backups or to a new phone.
- **Android:** an update installs only when it is MYLE Passwords signed by MYLE.

### 🐛 Fixes
- The vault locks after the chosen time even when the phone slept in between.
- A master password changed on another device is taken in only when it was made with your vault's key.
- A login with the same website twice no longer stops the vault's page.
- Signing out on the PC no longer signs you out here.
